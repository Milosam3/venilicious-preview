Hunter's guide

How to keep game meat cold on a hunting trip

A fridge trailer does the heavy lifting, but a few habits make a big difference to the meat you bring home.

  • Switch on earlyPower the trailer up a few hours before the first carcass goes in, so it starts cold.
  • Get carcasses in quicklyThe sooner the meat is cooling after the hunt, the better it keeps and the better it tastes.
  • Give it airSpace carcasses so cold air can move around them. Packed tight, they cool slowly.
  • Keep the door shutEvery opening lets the cold out. Plan what you need before you open up.
  • Park in the shadeShade and level ground help the trailer hold its temperature, especially in the bushveld heat.
  • Plan the trip homeKeep it plugged in right up until you leave, then head straight to your butcher.

FAQ

How much does fridge trailer hire cost?

The Short Boy and Tall Boy are R750 a day, the 1 Tonner is R950 a day and the Double Tonner is R1,050 a day. Hiring for longer? Send us your dates and we'll quote you.

Which fridge trailer do I need?

The Short Boy and Tall Boy carry up to 390 kg, which suits smaller hunts and functions. The 1 Tonner carries up to 1,100 kg and the Double Tonner up to 2,080 kg for bigger hunts and events. Not sure? Tell us what you're storing and we'll help you choose.

How cold does the trailer get?

Anywhere from +10°C down to −16°C, so you can run it as a fridge or a freezer.

Can I hang carcasses in the trailer?

Yes. Every trailer has a meat rail for hanging, and galvanised shelving can be fitted on request.

What do I need to tow the trailer?

A vehicle with a tow bar and a working trailer light plug. The 1 Tonner and Double Tonner are braked trailers. Make sure your vehicle's towing capacity and your driver's licence code cover the trailer you choose.

What power does the fridge trailer need?

The compressor plugs into mains power or a generator, so it works at the farm, the venue or the house.

Can I take the trailer to a hunting farm outside Gauteng?

Yes. Hunters are our main customers, so farm trips are exactly what the trailer is for. Let us know where you're headed when you book.

Do you deliver the trailer?

Collection is from Johannesburg. Ask about delivery when you book and we'll let you know what's possible for your area.

Is there a deposit?

A refundable deposit may apply. We'll confirm the amount and how to pay it when you book.

Can I hire the fridge trailer for a wedding or function?

Yes. It keeps drinks, catering and desserts cold on site, frees up space for your caterer and keeps the bar cold all day.

How early should I book for hunting season?

As soon as your dates are set. Hunting season is our busiest time of year, so the earlier you book, the better.

Do you sell meat too?

Yes. Venilicious sells venison, game, lamb and braai packs, delivered cold across Johannesburg. Shop the range.
